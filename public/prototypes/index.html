Bruno's Games · design prototypes

Pick a shelf

Four ways to show the collection, all running on the same mock data. The console filter, search and sort carry over when you switch between them, so you can compare like for like.

Real cases, real sizes

Each platform has its own case: PS2's black DVD case, the blue PS4 Blu-ray, the red Switch case, the white 3DS case, the SNES cardboard box, and so on. Everything is drawn at its true relative size.

PC launchers

PC games show where they activate (Steam, Origin, EA app, Rockstar, Battle.net, GOG, Ubisoft) as a strip on the cover, a badge on the spine and a filter that appears when you pick PC.

Pick it up

Click any game and it flies off the shelf in 3D. Drag to turn it round, see the spine and back, and open it to find the disc, cartridge or manual.
