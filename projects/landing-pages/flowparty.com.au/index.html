Who we are

We understand the need to provide a space where people can have fun, make friends and enjoy a unique experience at each party. It is of utmost importance that our customers feel safe to be whoever they are, free from any and all prejudices with the ssame feeling as being among friends, or having a party in their own home.

FLOW is exactly that: a party that makes you feel at home that allows you to create connections / relationships and promote pride and support among the entire LGBTQIA+ community, with respect, but also defending and guaranteeing freedom of expression.

 

Mission

Our mission is to create a safe, inclusive and free environment for the LGBTQI+ community, free of any judgment or dismcrimination, where people can be who they are and who they want to be

LGBTQIAPN+

Our vision is to be the biggest brazilian LGBTQI+ party in Australia, bring a missing space for brazilian community, taking place in different parts of Australia

Values

Our main values are the commitment with the LGBTQI+ cause, allowing a respectful and tolerant space for our community

Timeline

2021 - 2023

  • April 2021

    1st edition - Flow Exclusive

    Launch Party for friends only. Flow was created aiming to provide a safe LGTBQIA+ space where people felt comfortable to be who they are.

    17/04/2021 | ~100 atendees

  • December 2021

    2nd edition - Flow Carnaval

    After many requests, we celebrated the end of lockdown with a carnaval party. It was the first time we had live bands as well.

    05/12/2021 | ~300 atendees

  • March 2022

    3rd edition - Flow Mardi Gras

    The biggest queer event of all year resulted in our biggest even so far. We had to stop selling tickets because of the club's capacity. Incredible vibe.

    04/03/2022 | ~400 atendees

  • May 2022

    4th edition - Flow Glow

    We took advantage of VIVID to create a themed party, glow in the dark with uv lights, make up artist, neon face painting, laser and video projections.

    20/05/2022 | ~250 atendees

  • July 2022

    5th edition - Flow Pankadao

    The favela inspired party. It was the quickest event to sell out and again we had to stop selling due to club capacity. This event reinforced the need for a bigger venue.

    22/07/2022 | ~350 atendees

  • May 2022

    6th edition - Flow Wonderland

    Inspired by Alice in Wonderland and the Halloween season, Flow Wonderland brought a completely different world, mixing the best of both worlds, achieving our record crowd of 500 people in the venue. With several attractions, live show, 2 environments and a very attractive decoration.

    15/10/2022 | ~500 atendees

  • December 2022

    7th edition - Flow Carnaval

    After a fun-packed year, Flow was back for one last hoorah for 2022! And what better way to celebrate the huge year we had and all of our amazing patrons than with a FREE DAY PARTY featuring the best Brazilian Carnaval hits?

    12/12/2022 | ~400 atendees

  • March 2023

    8th edition - Flow World Pride

    Our World Pride event was held in the heart of Oxford Street at the Oxford Art Factory, counted with 7,5 hours of partying, shows, and live performances and 5 fabulous DJs.

    11/03/2023 | ~400 atendees

  • July 2023

    9th edition - Flow Pankadao II

    One of our best-received parties to date is coming back for its second edition and it will be even bigger and better than the first one.

  • October 2023

    10th edition - Flow Halloween

    Our Halloween event was held in the heart of Oxford Street at Kinselas Hotel, counted with 7,5 hours of partying, shows, and live performances and 5 fabulous DJs.

    14/10/2023 | ~500 atendees

  • February 2024

    11th edition - Flow metAMORphosis

    First time having Flow Party held at Arq Sydney, this was a Mardi Gras future themed party counting with circus artists and one after party making it an almost 12 hours party!

    16/02/2024 | ~400 atendees
