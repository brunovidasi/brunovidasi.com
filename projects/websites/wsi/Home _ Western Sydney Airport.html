Western Sydney

A hub of vibrant culture and thriving business, Western Sydney is home to a proudly diverse population of more than two million people.

The airport

Western Sydney International will be a world-class smart airport, built to service 10 million passengers a year from the day we open in 2026.

The vision

Western Sydney International will become the gateway of choice for Sydney, Australia and the world. It will be a hub for Australian and international visitors, a beacon for growing businesses and careers, and a global benchmark for air travel- proudly home in Western Sydney.

Find out more

Local opportunities

Western Sydney International will introduce thousands of job opportunities by 2031, and we look forward to collaborating with local businesses where possible. 

Find out more

A business hub

Our large-scale business park, located on the airport site, will offer growth opportunities to businesses at Western Sydney International.

New connections

The airport will be a catalyst for major new infrastructure in Western Sydney, designed to make Western Sydney International highly accessible. Travel to, from and around the area will be made even easier with new motorways, train lines and upgraded roads.

Your airport

The communities of Western Sydney are central to our identity, so the people of Western Sydney are central to our success. The airport is being designed to deliver many benefits for Western Sydney, and we will keep you up-to-date as we make progress.
